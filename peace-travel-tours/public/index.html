About us

A local agency that treats you like family

Peace Travel and Tours is a Bangladeshi-owned travel agency based in Elmhurst, Queens. Led by CEO Mahbubur Rahman, we've helped the local community and travelers across NYC book international and domestic tickets, Umrah visas, and Hajj packages at prices that make sense.

Rather than list prices that change daily, we ask for your travel details once — then Mahbubur checks current fares and reaches out to you directly with real options.

How it works

1. Create a free account and tell us your trip details.

2. We check current prices for your dates and destination.

3. You get an email confirming we're on it — then a follow-up with pricing and options.
